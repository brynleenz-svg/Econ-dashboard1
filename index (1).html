Where the figures come from

  • NZ dollar and Official Cash Rate: Reserve Bank of New Zealand (tables B1 and B2), updated each business day.
  • CPI, annual inflation and unemployment: Stats NZ, as republished by the Reserve Bank (tables M1 and M9), updated each quarter.

The "latest data" date on each card is the date the figure refers to, not the date we checked. Quarterly figures change only a few times a year.

Data: Stats NZ (licensed for re-use under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International) and the Reserve Bank of New Zealand. See the RBNZ terms of use.
